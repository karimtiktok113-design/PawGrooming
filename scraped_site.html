Backup & Restore

All data lives in this browser. Download a backup regularly and keep it safe — it's how you move to a new phone or laptop.

Restore replaces everything with the backup. Merge keeps your current data and only adds records from the backup that aren't already here — nothing is overwritten. Great for syncing your phone and laptop.
